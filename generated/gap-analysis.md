## Entry format

<a id="gap-s{stage}-{sequence}"></a>
### GAP-S{stage}-{sequence} — Short summary

- Status: open
- Type: missing-field | missing-context | ambiguity | conflict | dependency
- Source: Requirement ID and document page or section
- Affected artifacts: Paths or artifact IDs
- Missing fields or context: Exact details that are absent or unclear
- Impact: What cannot be specified, generated, or validated
- Decision needed: Precise question requiring an answer
- Owner: Person or role expected to answer
- Resolution: Pending
